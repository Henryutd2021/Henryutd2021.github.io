Field Notes

Large new loads and clean firm power: briefings, perspectives and practical data guides

Field Notes by Honglin Li: sourced briefings and perspectives on AI data-center load, nuclear and SMRs, hydrogen, electricity markets and the data and tools for modeling them.

Notes from the intersection I work on: large new electricity loads such as AI data centers and electrolyzers, and the clean firm power that could serve them. Briefings pick a few developments every two weeks and say why they matter for planning and modeling. The Data & Tools guide collects the public data and software I use, with the pitfalls I have run into. Perspectives, one claim at a time, will follow.
