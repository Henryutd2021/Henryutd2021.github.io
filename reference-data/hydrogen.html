Reference data / Hydrogen

Source-specific hydrogen techno-economic parameters and inputs, preserving printed values, technical and monetary bases, source terms and paper cross-references. Includes historical status, design coefficients, targets, policy inputs, an observed retail price and a clearly labeled paper model assumption.

Version 0.1 Checked 2026-11-13 76 quantities

Download versioned CSV How to cite

U.S. focus, with the global IRENA synthesis labeled separately. Coverage: 2007/2031. 2007 cost bases through the 2031 Hydrogen Shot goal, including historical status and 2026 policy and market inputs. Ultimate technology targets are undated.

Not a harmonized forecast: values keep their sources’ bases. NR means not reported; BOL means beginning of life; NOAK means nth-of-a-kind. Blank numeric CSV fields are missing values, never zero. Records with suffixes separate quantities or applicable years while keeping their common H-number. Costs have not been escalated.

“Paper use or numerical correspondence” distinguishes a stated model assumption from a matching number. A matching number alone does not establish adoption of a source or technology classification. All expanded basis and locator text is present in the page and the downloads.

S14 · DocumentG—

Read before using

Installed versus uninstalled
Equipment, installed direct cost, total installed capital (TIC), total overnight cost (TOC) and total capital investment (TCI) have different inclusions. Apply installation factors only after checking the boundary.
Stack versus system
Stack cost is part of system cost. Rectification and auxiliaries change electricity use; keep AC system input separate from DC stack ratings.
LHV versus HHV
Consumption in kWh/kg is a physical ratio; lower or higher heating value defines an efficiency percentage. Electrical-only SOEC efficiency retains an additional heat input.
Capacity versus output
Cost per kilogram stored, per kg/day of production capacity and per kilogram produced are different quantities.
Replacement versus O&M
Check replacement timing and inclusions before combining annual maintenance with per-replacement fractions or replacement-inclusive O&M.
Capture versus lifecycle
Process-carbon capture does not remove upstream methane, purchased-electricity or all combustion emissions. H52 is a reference-plant coefficient, not a lifecycle estimate.
Policy eligibility versus assumed support
A model’s support assumption does not establish statutory eligibility. Apply the production-year amount and the qualifying conditions together.
Production cost versus sale price
Hydrogen Shot is a production-cost goal. Retail prices include downstream services; neither is automatically a plant-gate sale price.

Gaps

Public-source coverage still needed for comparable inputs:

ATR without CCS
The cited NETL report has no such case. Subtracting capture equipment from ATR+CCS does not supply a complete unabated design.
Unit-level CCS removal assumptions
NETL’s overall capture objective does not supply a universal solvent-removal input.
Before-capture intensity of capture-equipped plants
The unabated SMR coefficient H52 should not be transferred to SMR+CCS or ATR+CCS; their fuel and process balances differ.
Current PEM/alkaline variable O&M
A comparable public benchmark excluding electricity, water and stack replacement is not established here. H27 is a model factor.
Current MW-scale, continuous-duty pure-hydrogen fuel cells
