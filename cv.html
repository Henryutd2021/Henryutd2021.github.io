Internships

Feb 2025 – Aug 2025

Idaho National Laboratory (INL)

  • Optimization of Hydrogen Integration in Nuclear Grid Services: Developed an optimization framework using mathematical programming techniques to identify the most profitable deployment strategies for nuclear-powered hydrogen production. Integrated techno-economic analysis, historical market data (ISO prices), and policy incentives (45V tax credits) to maximize revenue from hydrogen sales and grid ancillary services; results published in Nature Communications (2026, first author).
  • Techno-Economic Analysis of Non-Powered Dam Retrofits: Contributed a pro-forma techno-economic module to an INL toolset for retrofitting U.S. non-powered dams with hydropower and battery storage; integration with floating PV was scoped in planning.

Aug 2024 – Jan 2025

FM

  • Solar PV Microgrid Design Optimization: Developed and implemented a techno-economic model incorporating optimization techniques to determine the optimal sizing of solar PV, battery storage, and backup generation for an industrial research campus microgrid. Minimized levelized cost of energy subject to meeting demand in modeled grid-outage scenarios, using a DOE reference load profile scaled to the campus’s annual consumption and site solar resource data.
  • Lithium Battery Production Line Simulation and Bottleneck Optimization: Built a discrete-event simulation model (Arena) of a lithium battery production line to quantify the economic impact of equipment failures and maintenance schedules. Identified production bottlenecks and analyzed potential improvements through simulation, implicitly informing optimization of maintenance and buffer strategies.
  • Wind Farm Maintenance Strategy Optimization: Proposed a research framework featuring an optimization model to determine the optimal predictive and corrective maintenance scheduling for wind farms. Aimed at balancing O&M costs, maximizing energy production (reducing downtime), and ensuring long-term asset reliability.
