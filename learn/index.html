Energy Systems, Step by Step

Free interactive lessons on energy systems by Honglin Li: run a small town’s power grid, then learn what electricity costs and how to build and question optimization models, in tiers from beginner to graduate level.

One small fictional town and a power grid you run yourself. Start with a game, then climb as far as you like: from how the grid works to building and questioning your own models.
